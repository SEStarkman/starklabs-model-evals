# Case 5: Interesting Fact

## Instructions for the candidate model

Find one fact that Sam Starkman would find interesting and likely doesn't already know.

### Research His Interests

Study Sam's public presence to understand his interests. Read from ALL THREE of these sources:

1. **X (Twitter):** https://x.com/sam_starkman
2. **Website:** https://samstarkman.com (especially the `/writing` section)
3. **Newsletter:** https://themetagame.beehiiv.com

Note: Some newsletter posts are also featured on the website under `/writing`. Do not double-count duplicated material.

### The Fact

Present one fact that:
- Is credible and sourced with at least 2 reputable references
- Aligns with his demonstrated interests (technology, AI, psychology, game theory, poker, security, systems thinking, or adjacent domains)
- Is plausibly new to him — not something he's already written about publicly
- Is genuinely interesting, not just obscure trivia

### Output

Provide:
1. The fact itself, stated clearly
2. Why you think he'd find it interesting (reference specific interests you observed)
3. At least 2 credible sources supporting the fact
4. Why you believe he likely doesn't already know this
