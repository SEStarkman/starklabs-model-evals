# Case 4: Long-Form Model X Post

## Instructions for the candidate model

Write a long-form educational post about the AI model you are currently running on. This is a meta-task: you are writing about yourself.

### Research

First, research your own model. Find and use credible sources to cover:
- The official release date and organization behind it
- Architecture details (parameter count, training approach, context window)
- Key capabilities and benchmark results
- Known limitations or weaknesses
- How it compares meaningfully to contemporaneous models in its class

### Voice Calibration

Before writing, study the public work of Sam Starkman to understand his voice. Read from ALL THREE of these sources:

1. **X (Twitter):** https://x.com/sam_starkman
2. **Website:** https://samstarkman.com (especially the `/writing` section)
3. **Newsletter:** https://themetagame.beehiiv.com

Note: Some newsletter posts are also featured on the website under `/writing`. Do not double-count duplicated material.

From these sources, infer his writing style: structure, tone, humor level, sentence rhythm, how he handles technical topics, and what kinds of examples he uses.

### The Post

Write a long-form X thread (8-15 tweets) about your model that:

- Is informative and educational — someone should learn something concrete
- Has strong flow and structure from hook to conclusion
- Includes specific details (benchmarks, architecture, comparisons) not just vague praise
- Uses dry humor or sarcasm only when it flows naturally and sounds like the person whose voice you're writing in
- Reads like something Sam Starkman would actually post

### Output

Provide:
1. The final X thread, ready to post
2. Brief research notes with sources you used
3. A short note on what you inferred about the target voice and how you applied it
